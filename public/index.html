Good to know

Questions, answered

What happens after I book?

Your time is saved for you straight away. I'll then call, text or email you, whichever you chose, to confirm the details before your visit.

Can I change or cancel my time?

Of course. Call or text me at 647-629-6061 and I'll sort it out.

I'm in pain. What should I do?

Please call 647-629-6061. Calling is the fastest way to get help.

Can I book for my whole family?

Yes. Choose how many people when you book and add their names and ages in the note.

Do I need dental insurance?

No. Just let me know whether you have it and who it's with, and I can answer your questions when I follow up.

What happens to my information?

Your details are stored securely and are only used to arrange your visit. Please don't include private medical details when you book.
