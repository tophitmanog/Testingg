Good to know

Questions, answered

Is my appointment confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends me a request. I'll contact you by call, text or email, whichever you chose, to confirm a time.

I'm in pain. What should I do?

Please call 647-629-6061 rather than using the form. Calling is the fastest way to get help.

Can I book for my whole family?

Yes. Choose how many people in the form and add their names and ages in the note, and I'll try to book you together.

Do I need dental insurance?

No. Let me know if you have insurance and who it's with, and I can answer your questions when I get back to you.

What happens to my information?

Your details are only used to contact you about your request. Please don't include private medical details in the form.
